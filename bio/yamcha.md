Yamcha is a fictional character from the Dragon Ball manga and anime series, created by Akira Toriyama. He first appears as a desert bandit and an antagonist to Son Goku, accompanied by his lifelong best friend, the anthropomorphic cat Pu'ar. Initially portrayed with gynophobia (a fear of women), Yamcha eventually reforms and becomes a loyal ally to Goku and the other Z Fighters.

A skilled human martial artist, Yamcha's signature technique is the "Wolf Fang Fist" (Rōgafūfūken), a rapid flurry of punches and kicks. After training with Master Roshi, he developed superhuman abilities including super strength, speed, agility, flight, and the capacity to manipulate ki for energy projection and sensing. He was also a former boyfriend of Bulma.

While a significant character in the original Dragon Ball series, Yamcha's fighting abilities became largely outclassed by other characters, particularly the Saiyans, in Dragon Ball Z and Dragon Ball Super. Despite this, he remains a present figure, occasionally lending assistance when new threats emerge. Yamcha has received mixed reviews, often criticized for his comparative weakness but also praised as a fun and iconic character.
