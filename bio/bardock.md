Bardock is a low-class Saiyan warrior, the husband of Gine, and the biological father of Goku (Kakarot) and Raditz. He is primarily known as the protagonist of the 1990 TV special "Dragon Ball Z: Bardock – The Father of Goku," which serves as a prequel to the main Dragon Ball Z series.

After a mission, Bardock gains the ability of precognitive foresight, experiencing visions of Planet Vegeta's destruction at the hands of Frieza, the emperor overseeing the Saiyans, and his infant son Kakarot's future on Earth. Despite his efforts to warn his fellow Saiyans, his warnings are dismissed. Bardock then makes a desperate, solitary stand against Frieza's forces in orbit above Planet Vegeta, but is ultimately obliterated along with his home planet by Frieza's Supernova attack. Before his death, he has a final vision of Goku confronting Frieza, dying with a smile, knowing his son is destined to avenge their people.

While initially a character created by Toei Animation, Bardock's story and character have since been incorporated into the Dragon Ball canon. Later, in "Dragon Ball: Episode of Bardock," he is depicted as being sent back in time and becoming the first Super Saiyan after battling Frieza's ancestor, Chilled.
