Captain Ginyu is a fictional character from the Dragon Ball Z manga and anime, serving as the flamboyant leader of the elite Ginyu Force and a loyal henchman to Frieza. Known for his distinctive purple appearance, two horns, and an affinity for striking dramatic poses, Ginyu is a formidable warrior.

His signature ability is "Body Change," which allows him to swap bodies with an opponent, taking over their physical form and power. This ability was famously used when he exchanged bodies with Goku during the Frieza Saga on Planet Namek. After a series of events, Ginyu ultimately ended up trapped in the body of a Namekian frog for the remainder of the series. His name is a pun on the Japanese word for milk, "gyunyu". The body he is typically seen in is not his original form, as he has used his Body Change technique multiple times throughout his history.
