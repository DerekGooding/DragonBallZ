Kami is an old Namekian and the original guardian of Earth in Dragon Ball and Dragon Ball Z. Before becoming guardian, he separated the evil within himself to attain the position, which resulted in the birth of King Piccolo. Kami is also the creator of Earth's Dragon Balls and the eternal dragon, Shenron. He plays a key role in guiding Goku and other Z-Fighters, and eventually merges with Piccolo during the Android Saga, significantly increasing Piccolo's power and knowledge.
