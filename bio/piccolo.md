Piccolo is a prominent fictional character in the Dragon Ball media franchise, created by Akira Toriyama. He first appeared in Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine on February 9, 1988, as the reincarnation of the evil King Piccolo, initially serving as a demonic antagonist.

Originally born from the evil half of the Namekian who became Earth's God (Kami), Piccolo Jr. inherited his father's memories and a desire for revenge against Goku, who had defeated King Piccolo. However, his character undergoes significant development throughout the series. He gradually sheds his villainous traits, particularly after he begins training Goku's son, Gohan, during the Saiyan Saga. This training fosters a strong bond between Piccolo and Gohan, leading Piccolo to become a permanent member of the Dragon Team and one of Earth's greatest heroes.

Piccolo is known for being a wise, cunning, and independent warrior who often keeps to himself. Despite his reserved nature, he is fiercely protective of his friends and has sacrificed himself on multiple occasions for the greater good. He is a Namekian, an extraterrestrial humanoid species from the planet Namek, known for their ability to create Dragon Balls.
