Master Roshi, also known as the "Turtle Hermit" (Kame Sennin) or "Old Master of Martial Arts" (Muten Rōshi), is a prominent fictional character from the Dragon Ball manga and anime series, created by Akira Toriyama. He is an ancient and wise martial arts master, said to be over 300 years old, who resides on a small island called Kame House with his pet sea turtle.

Roshi is renowned as the inventor of the iconic Kamehameha technique, a powerful energy blast. He has trained many of Earth's greatest fighters, including Goku, Krillin, Yamcha, Grandpa Gohan, and the Ox-King. Despite his profound wisdom and martial arts prowess, Master Roshi is also characterized by his perverted antics and often serves as comic relief in the series. He frequently flirts with attractive female characters, which sometimes leads to humorous situations.

During martial arts tournaments, he often competes anonymously under the disguise of "Jackie Chun" to teach his students humility and prevent them from becoming arrogant. While his power level was initially considered the strongest among mortals in the Dragon Ball universe, it was later surpassed by his students. However, he remains a formidable fighter and even participated in the Tournament of Power in Dragon Ball Super, demonstrating his continued relevance and skill.
