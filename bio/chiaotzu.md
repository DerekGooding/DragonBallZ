Chiaotzu is a small, pale-skinned human character from the Dragon Ball series, visually resembling a jiangshi with red cheeks and a single hair beneath his hat. He is characterized by his psychokinetic and telepathic abilities, rather than physical prowess.

He was first introduced at the 22nd World Martial Arts Tournament as a student of Master Shen, a rival of Master Roshi. Alongside his close companion Tien Shinhan, Chiaotzu initially operated as a con artist and trained as a mercenary under Master Shen, often using his mental powers to cheat, such as manipulating tournament match orders.

After Tien Shinhan recognized the flaws in Master Shen's teachings, both he and Chiaotzu abandoned their former master to join Goku and his allies. Throughout the series, Chiaotzu has faced death on multiple occasions, including being killed by King Piccolo while attempting to thwart his wish for eternal youth, and later sacrificing himself by self-destructing on Nappa to protect Tien Shinhan. He was subsequently brought back to life using the Dragon Balls. In later parts of the story, he frequently assists Tien Shinhan, even employing his telekinetic abilities for tasks like farming.
