Son Gohan is a fictional character from the Japanese Dragon Ball franchise, created by Akira Toriyama. He is the elder son of the protagonist Son Goku and his wife Chi-Chi, and the older brother of Goten.

Introduced in Weekly Shōnen Jump on October 8, 1988, Gohan is a Saiyan-human hybrid. Unlike his father, Gohan initially lacks a passion for fighting and is depicted as a shy, well-mannered, and studious intellectual. His mother, Chi-Chi, often pushed him to focus on academics, forbidding him from martial arts.

Despite his scholarly aspirations, various threats to Earth force Gohan to fight, revealing his immense hidden potential, which often awakens in moments of extreme rage or distress. He is trained by his father's former nemesis, Piccolo, and later by Goku himself. Gohan becomes one of the strongest characters in the series, notably being the first Saiyan in the series to achieve the Super Saiyan 2 transformation during his battle against Cell.

As an adult, Gohan achieves his dream of becoming a scholar, though he continues to fight alongside the Z Fighters to defend Earth when necessary. He is married to Videl and has a daughter named Pan.
